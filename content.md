## 5. Technology & Manufacturing (Image 7, 5, 2, 12)
*   **Vibro Casting Process**: Moving away from the traditional method (Spun Technology), the plant is equipped with the latest Concrete pipes manufacturing Process (Vertical Vibro casting manufacturing process) with its own Automated Concrete batching plant. Gives RCC Vibrate Casting Pipes a great durability, Smooth surface without cracks and longer life.
*   **Concrete Batching Plant**: Equipped with American made Apollo's Hawk-eye Pedershaap Concrete Batching plant. Concrete is produced by mixing Sand, Water, gravel, and Cement under controlled conditions taking into account standard production procedures.
*   **Vibro Core Vibration**: Ensures uniform compaction.
*   **Rubber Ring Joint**: Provides deviation flexibility.

### Why Vertical Vibro Casting? (Comparison Matrix)
*   **Homogeneous compaction** & Uniform material distribution.
*   **Uniform crushing strength** at jacking surfaces.
*   **Uniform concrete at surface** – no cosmetic cracking.
*   **No leakage** and resulting stony surface areas.
*   **Spigot receives efficient dry cast** pressing & compaction.
*   **Water cement ratio**: Low at approx .25 - .30
*   **Slumping**: No slumping
*   **Variability**: High and transparent
*   **Compressive strength**: Resulting from low and constant water cement ratio.
*   **Wall thickness**: Heavier walled pipe providing increased cover and longer design life.
*   **Capacity**: Production of large numbers of pipes possible per day.
*   **Pipe tolerances**: Excellent and consistent tolerance control due to automated process.
*   **Liner locking system**: Can take any liner locking system.

## 7. Quality Assurance (Image 6 & 12)
Follows strict quality checks from planning stage to delivery stage.
*   **Raw Material Examination**: Cement, steel, and sand procured from established vendors and inspected thoroughly upon arrival. Sent to automated batching plant only after approval.
*   **In Process Quality Control**: Manufacturing under strict supervision of highly qualified staffs. Variety of tests conducted.
*   **Final Testing**: Rigorous testing for every batch of products at random to ensure international standard compliance.
*   **Testing Equipments**:
    *   **Three Edge Bearing Load Test**: Conducted by applying load on the pipe through a jack until the pipe gets cracked. Pipe passes if load conditions per IS specification are satisfied.
    *   **Hydro Test**: Conducted by applying water pressure from a pump to the pipe covered on all sides to determine the pressure it withstands before leaking. Approved if compatible with ISI specifications.
